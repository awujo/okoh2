General Questions

What is NexoraCapitals?

NexoraCapitals is an algorithmic trading platform that uses advanced technology to trade cryptocurrencies and commodities. Our system analyzes market data 24/7 to identify profitable trading opportunities.

How does algorithmic trading work?

Our algorithms automatically analyze market conditions, execute trades, and manage risk based on predefined strategies. This removes emotional decision-making and can operate much faster than human traders.

Is my investment secure?

We implement multiple security measures including encryption, cold storage for funds, and two-factor authentication. However, all trading involves risk, and past performance is not indicative of future results.

Account & Investments

How do I create an account?

Click "Get Started" in the top navigation and complete the registration form. You'll need to provide some basic information and verify your email address.

What is the minimum investment amount?

The minimum investment is $200 for our Starter Plan. Higher-tier plans have larger minimum investments but offer better returns.

How are profits calculated and paid?

Profits are calculated daily based on your investment plan's ROI percentage and distributed weekly. You can withdraw these profits or reinvest them.

Trading & Strategies

What markets do you trade in?

Our algorithms primarily trade in cryptocurrency markets (BTC, ETH, etc.) and commodities (gold, oil, etc.), with some exposure to forex markets for diversification.

What trading strategies do you use?

We employ multiple strategies including trend following, arbitrage, market making, and mean reversion. Our system automatically selects the most appropriate strategy based on market conditions.

Can I withdraw my initial investment?

Your initial investment is locked for the duration of your chosen plan (typically 31 days). After this period, you can withdraw both your principal and accumulated profits.
