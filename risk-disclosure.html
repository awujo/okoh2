General Risk Disclosure

Trading cryptocurrencies, commodities, and other financial instruments carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. Before deciding to trade, you should carefully consider your investment objectives, level of experience, and risk appetite.

Potential Risks Include:

  • Market Risk: Prices can fluctuate rapidly due to market conditions, news events, and other factors beyond our control.
  • Liquidity Risk: Some markets may become illiquid, making it difficult to execute trades at desired prices.
  • Leverage Risk: The use of leverage can work against you as well as for you and can lead to large losses as well as gains.
  • Technology Risk: System failures, connectivity issues, or other technical problems may prevent orders from being executed.
  • Algorithm Risk: While our algorithms are designed to perform in various market conditions, they may not perform as expected.

Algorithmic Trading Specific Risks

Algorithmic trading involves additional risks beyond traditional trading:

Strategy Risks

All trading strategies have inherent weaknesses that may be exposed during certain market conditions. Our algorithms include risk management protocols, but cannot eliminate risk entirely.

Over-Optimization Risk

Strategies that perform well in historical testing may not perform as well in live markets due to changing conditions.

Black Swan Events

Extreme market events can cause strategies to behave in unexpected ways, potentially resulting in significant losses.

Investment Performance

Past performance is not necessarily indicative of future results. The performance figures provided are based on backtesting and/or actual trading history, but are not guarantees of future performance.
