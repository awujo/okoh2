frequently asked questions

Find answers to common questions about our trading platform and investment process.

Our proprietary algorithms analyze market data 24/7, identifying patterns and executing trades at optimal moments. The system combines technical indicators, volume analysis, and machine learning to maximize returns while managing risk.

We accept Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT (ERC-20), and other major cryptocurrencies. All investments are converted to USD value at the time of deposit for calculation purposes.

Returns are calculated daily based on your investment plan and paid weekly directly to your North Bridge Investments account. You can withdraw your earnings or reinvest them for compound growth.

The minimum investment amount is $200 for our Starter Plan. Higher-tier plans have larger minimums but offer better returns.

We use enterprise-grade security measures including cold storage for funds, two-factor authentication, and regular security audits to protect your investments.

You can withdraw your earnings at any time. Principal investments are locked for the duration of your chosen plan (typically 31 days), after which they become available for withdrawal.
