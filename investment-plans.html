Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our investment plans

How do I start investing with North Bridge Investments?

Starting is simple: 1) Create an account, 2) Deposit funds using cryptocurrency or other payment methods, 3) Choose an investment plan, and 4) Watch your investment grow through our algorithmic trading strategies.

What is the minimum investment amount?

The minimum investment amount is $200 for our Starter Plan. Higher-tier plans have larger minimum investments but offer better returns and additional benefits.

How are profits calculated and distributed?

Profits are calculated daily based on your investment plan's ROI percentage and distributed to your account weekly. You can withdraw these profits or reinvest them for compound growth.

Can I withdraw my initial investment?

Your initial investment is locked for the duration of your chosen plan (typically 31 days). After this period, you can withdraw both your principal and accumulated profits.

What markets do you trade in?

Our algorithms primarily trade in cryptocurrency markets (BTC, ETH, etc.) and commodities (gold, oil, etc.), with some exposure to forex markets for diversification.
